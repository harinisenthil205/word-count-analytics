Big data analytics helps organizations understand large collections of information.
Word count is a simple example of text analytics. A word count application reads text,
splits text into words, and counts how often each word appears. Big data systems can
process large datasets by dividing work across multiple computers. The map step emits
word pairs, and the reduce step adds the values for each word. Word count is useful for
search engines, document analysis, customer feedback, and social media analytics.
